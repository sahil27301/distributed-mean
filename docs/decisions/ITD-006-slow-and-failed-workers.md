| ITD 6 - *Use pull-based claiming with short heartbeated leases to handle slow and crashed workers* |  |
| :---- | :---- |
| **THE PROBLEM** | How do we keep slow or dead workers from stalling jobs, without knowing worker speeds in advance? |
| **OPTIONS CONSIDERED (Decision in bold)** | ***Workers pull work; 30 s lease renewed every 10 s by heartbeat; backup copies deferred*** <br> Static assignment of tasks to workers up front <br> Speed-aware routing (track speeds, keep slow workers off critical tasks) <br> Long fixed lease (e.g. 15 min) without heartbeat |
| **REASONING** | Pulling self-balances: fast workers come back sooner and take more, with no speed knowledge. <br> Static assignment makes the job as slow as the slowest worker's share. <br> Speed-aware routing only helps when slowness is persistent per worker (not for random pauses) and needs a central router. <br> Short lease + heartbeat: a crashed worker's items are reclaimable within ~30 s, while a slow but alive worker keeps its items. A long fixed lease delays crash recovery. |
| **TRADEOFFS** | Tail: if a slow worker holds a job's last task, the job waits for it. Backup copies would fix this and are already safe under ITD 3. <br> Heartbeats add ~W/10 writes per second. <br> A worker that pauses longer than the lease can lose its items; its work is wasted, but correctness holds (ITD 3). |
| **NOTES** | Simulation: per-worker speed factor × random jitter × cost proportional to files × C. <br> A crash right after claiming also costs the job its round-robin turn (`last_claimed_at` was already bumped, ITD 5): the job is delayed by at most one cycle plus one lease. |
